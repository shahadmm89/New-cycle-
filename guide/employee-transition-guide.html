What is Retroactive Payment?

During January, February and March 2027, eligible payments are first calculated using the old basic salary.

When the new salary takes effect in April, the difference between the old and new basic salary amounts is calculated and paid retroactively in April.

You don't lose the difference — you receive it in April.

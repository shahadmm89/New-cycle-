## Full script, uninterrupted

Did you know our Salary Cycle is changing? Until now, it has followed a January-to-December cycle. For merit, recommendations are based on projected market salary movement. Bonus is based on estimated company performance across key KPIs - HSE, Finance, and Performance. Now, we're moving to a new cycle. From April to March. Under the new cycle, merit increases and promotions take effect on April 1. Bonus will be paid in the March payroll. Your performance appraisal remains on the same schedule, and will continue to close in December. This change brings our cycle closer to market best practices, and gives us more relevant information when making salary decisions. Have questions? HR is ready to help.
